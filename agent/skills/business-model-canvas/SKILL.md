# Business Model Canvas - Structured Business Model Analysis

LLM-powered Business Model Canvas generation and analysis following
the Osterwalder framework. Breaks down any company or startup idea
into nine building blocks.

## Capabilities
- Generate complete Business Model Canvas for any company
- Analyze and critique existing business models
- Compare business models across competitors
- Identify innovation opportunities in business model

## Parameters
- `company`: Company to analyze
- `idea`: Startup idea to model (alternative to company)
- `action`: "generate", "analyze", "compare"
- `companies`: List of companies for comparison mode
