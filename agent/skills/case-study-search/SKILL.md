# Case Study Search - Business School Case Finder

Search for and summarize relevant business school case studies from academic
databases and open sources. Maps research topics to classic and contemporary
business cases.

## Capabilities
- Search for case studies by company, industry, or topic
- Identify relevant Harvard/Kellogg/Stanford/Darden cases
- Map topics to classic business school frameworks
- Suggest case-based teaching approaches
- Connect current events to established case studies

## Parameters
- `query`: Search query (company, topic, or framework)
- `action`: "search", "frameworks", "suggest_cases"
- `industry`: Filter by industry
