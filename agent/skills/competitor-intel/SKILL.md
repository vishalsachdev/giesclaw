# Competitive Intelligence - Competitor Analysis & Benchmarking

Gather and analyze competitive intelligence on companies and their rivals.
Combines financial data with LLM-powered strategic analysis.

## Capabilities
- Identify key competitors for any company
- Compare financial metrics across competitors
- Analyze competitive positioning
- Track competitive dynamics over time

## Parameters
- `company`: Target company (ticker or name)
- `competitors`: List of competitor tickers (auto-detected if omitted)
- `action`: "identify", "compare", "positioning"
