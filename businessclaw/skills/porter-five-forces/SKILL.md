# Porter's Five Forces Analysis

LLM-powered Porter's Five Forces framework analysis for any industry or company.
Evaluates competitive intensity through the five forces: competitive rivalry,
supplier power, buyer power, threat of substitutes, and threat of new entrants.

## Capabilities
- Analyze competitive dynamics of any industry
- Rate each force on a scale (low/medium/high)
- Identify key factors driving each force
- Generate strategic implications and recommendations
- Compare industry attractiveness across sectors

## Parameters
- `company`: Company to analyze (e.g., "Apple")
- `industry`: Industry to analyze (e.g., "smartphone manufacturing")
- `detail_level`: "brief" or "detailed" (default: "detailed")
