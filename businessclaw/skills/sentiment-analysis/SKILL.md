# Sentiment Analysis - Brand & Market Sentiment

Analyze sentiment of text data including earnings call transcripts,
news articles, social media, and customer reviews.

## Capabilities
- Analyze sentiment polarity and subjectivity
- Process earnings call transcripts for tone
- Evaluate brand sentiment from text
- Compare sentiment across companies or time periods

## Parameters
- `text`: Text to analyze
- `texts`: List of texts for batch analysis
- `action`: "analyze", "compare", "earnings_tone"
- `labels`: Optional labels for each text
